## Blokada 5 for Android

[Blokada 5](/blokada-5/) is the older app. It blocks ads and trackers in every app and browser on your Android phone or tablet. It is free, open source, and needs no account and no root.

<p><a class="btn btn-outline" href="https://go.blokada.org/apk5?src=landing">Download Blokada 5 (.apk)</a></p>

It runs on Android 5 and newer.

## Why Blokada 5 is not on Google Play

Google Play does not allow apps that block ads in other apps, so Blokada 5 cannot be published there. The file on this page is the official one, built from the [public source code](https://github.com/blokadaorg/five-android).

Download it only from blokada.org. Copies on other apk sites may be out of date or changed.

## How to install Blokada 5

1. Tap the download button above on your Android device.
2. Open the downloaded file. You find it in your browser's downloads or in the *Files* app.
3. Android asks whether this app may install apps. Tap *Settings*, turn on *Allow from this source*, and go back.
4. Tap *Install*, then *Open*.
5. Tap the power button in Blokada. Android asks to set up a VPN connection: tap *OK*.

Blokada 5 uses Android's VPN feature to filter ads on the device itself. Your traffic is not sent to a VPN server.
